核心承诺：我们不收集你的任何个人数据

SayPlain（以下简称"本应用"）是一款完全在设备端运行的 AI 文档解读工具。本应用不收集、不存储、不上传、不共享任何个人数据。你在应用内拍摄或导入的合同、条款、报价单、报告等文件，全部内容仅在你的设备上处理，不会离开这台设备。

一、我们如何处理你导入的内容

  • 文字提取：照片中的文字通过设备内置的 OCR 能力（Apple Vision 框架）在本机识别。
  • AI 解读：解读由运行在你设备上的 Apple 端侧大语言模型（Apple Foundation Models）完成。内容不会上传至我们或任何第三方的服务器。
  • 结果保存：解读结果（含你导入文件的缩略图与文字摘录）默认仅保存在本机（iOS 应用沙盒内）。
  • 本应用没有账号系统，不要求你提供姓名、邮箱、电话号码或任何身份信息。

  • Apple App Store（StoreKit）：购买、订阅与恢复购买由 Apple 支付体系处理。Apple 会按其隐私政策处理交易数据；本应用开发者无法看到你的卡号、账单地址等支付信息。
  • 界面开源组件：应用内使用了不收集任何数据的开源界面布局组件（SnapKit、IQKeyboardManager）。
  • 本应用不含任何广告 SDK、统计分析工具或追踪代码，不使用第三方 Cookie 追踪。

本应用不针对 13 周岁以下儿童设计，我们不会主动收集任何用户的个人数据。

Our Core Promise: We Collect No Personal Data

SayPlain is an AI document-interpretation app that runs entirely on your device. SayPlain does not collect, store, upload, or share any personal data. Contracts, terms, quotes, reports, and any other files you photograph or import are processed solely on your device and never leave it.

Last updated: October 24, 2026 ｜ Effective: October 24, 2026

1. How Your Imported Content Is Processed

  • Text extraction: text in photos is recognized on-device via Apple's Vision framework (OCR).
  • AI interpretation: performed by Apple's on-device Foundation Models running locally on your iPhone. Your content is never uploaded to us or any third-party server.
  • Results storage: interpretation results (including thumbnails and text excerpts of your files) are stored only inside the app's sandbox on your device.
  • There is no account system. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, or any identifying information.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store (StoreKit): purchases, subscriptions, and restores are processed by Apple Pay. Apple handles transaction data under its own privacy policy. The developer cannot see your card number or billing address.
  • Open-source UI components: the app includes open-source layout components (SnapKit, IQKeyboardManager) that collect no data whatsoever.
  • The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics, and no tracking code. We do not use third-party cookies for tracking.

4. Children's Privacy

SayPlain is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from any user.

5. Data Deletion & Export

  • You may export all interpretation records (JSON) or wipe all local data at any time from Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes all sandboxed data (except the free-quota counter; see Section 2).
  • Because we collect and transmit nothing, there is no data on any server to delete — we have no servers.

6. Changes to This Policy

Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and on our App Store product page. Material changes will be announced in-app before taking effect.
